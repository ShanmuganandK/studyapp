# Play Console — Data Safety form + Designed-for-Families answers
**Re: "Tinku Math" — the exact answers to enter in Play Console, and why each one is defensible**

_Prepared 2026-08-15. Tracks TRACKER "Now" #2. Pairs with the policy itself, whose text lives in
`src/config/privacyPolicy.js` and is published at `/privacy.html`._

## How to use this document

Play Console's Data Safety form is a branching wizard: most sections disappear once you answer
"No" at the top. This file gives **the answer to type, verbatim**, in wizard order, plus the
factual basis for each — so that if the answers are ever challenged (by Play review, by a parent,
or by counsel) the reasoning is recoverable rather than remembered.

**Rule for this file: it is only as true as the build.** Every "No" below rests on the network
audit of 2026-08-15 (zero off-origin requests from the built app) and on the guard tests that
keep it that way. **If a future build adds a network call, an SDK, or an account, this form must
be re-answered before that build ships** — Play treats an inaccurate Data Safety declaration as a
policy violation in its own right, independent of whether the data practice itself was allowed.

## §0 — Factual basis (read before answering; every answer below depends on it)

1. **No accounts, no sign-up, no anonymous UID.** The auth seam is an inert null-user adapter;
   the `firebase` dependency, `lib/firebase.js` and `firebaseAdapter.js` were deleted 2026-08-15
   (DECISIONS 2026-08-15). No auth SDK ships.
2. **No analytics, no telemetry, no crash SDK of our own.** The analytics wrapper is an inert
   no-op seam (DECISIONS 2026-07-16). No Firebase Analytics, no `gtag`, no third-party SDK.
3. **No ads, no ad SDK, no ad network, no third-party marketing tooling.**
4. **No network calls originate from the app.** Measured, not inferred: the built app loaded in a
   real browser issues **12 requests, all same-origin, zero off-origin**. Standing guard:
   `src/services/__tests__/noFirebaseAuth.test.js`.
5. **Fonts are self-hosted** (Fontsource woff2 bundled and served same-origin). No Google Fonts,
   no CDN.
6. **All progress is device-local**, written by `services/progressStore` under the key
   `tinku:v1:skills`. Never keyed to a user. Never transmitted.
7. **The one outbound link** is a parent-initiated WhatsApp feedback link (`wa.me`) in the parent
   zone. It is a link the parent chooses to tap, which hands off to WhatsApp; the app sends
   nothing. See §3 for how this is declared.
8. **What we DO receive, automatically and outside our control:** Play Console vitals
   (crash/ANR/install data, aggregated by Google) and Netlify request logs for the web/PWA build
   (IP, user agent). Neither is collected *by the app*, and neither is what the Data Safety form
   is asking about — see §2.

### The trap in this form

Play's Data Safety question is scoped to **data your app collects or shares** — including data
collected by any SDK in the app, and data your servers receive. It is **not** asking about:

- data Google itself gathers as the distribution platform (Play Console vitals), or
- standard server logs generated by delivering the app or the web build.

That is why "No" is correct on the form while the privacy policy still carries the "Standard
technical information" section. **The two are answering different questions and must both stay as
they are.** Do not "reconcile" them by adding a data type to the form, and do not reconcile them
by deleting the technical-information section from the policy — DECISIONS 2026-08-14 rejects both.

## §2 — Why the policy says more than the form

The privacy policy acknowledges that the hosting provider and app store record standard technical
information such as IP address and crash reports. Someone comparing the two documents may ask why
the form says "No collection".

**Recorded answer:** the app collects nothing — that is the flat, measured claim (§0.4). The
hosting provider and the store are the *delivery services*, and their logs are theirs, generated
by the act of delivery, not by the app's behaviour, and not under our control. The policy
discloses them because a parent deserves the whole picture; the form does not list them because
the form asks a narrower question. The absolute claim "we collect no personal data" is rejected
precisely so that this distinction stays honest (DECISIONS 2026-08-14, amended 2026-08-15).

## §3 — The WhatsApp feedback link

The parent zone contains a `wa.me` link that opens WhatsApp with a pre-filled subject line
("Tinku Math feedback: "). What to know when answering the form:

- The app **sends nothing**. Tapping the link hands off to WhatsApp or the browser; whatever the
  parent then chooses to type and send is a message from the parent to us, in WhatsApp.
- It is **parent-initiated**, in the parent zone, behind the parent gate. It is not reachable from
  any kid-facing screen.
- If we ever reply, we then hold a phone number and a message — **received**, not collected by the
  app. That is ordinary correspondence, not an app data practice.
- **Data Safety answer is unchanged: No collection.**
- ⚠️ **Designed for Families check this against:** the programme's rules on links out of the app.
  The link leaves the app, so it must stay behind the parent gate (it is) and must not be
  presented to children (it is not). Confirm the current DFF wording on external links before
  submitting — see §4.

We target under-13s, so the programme applies. This task settles the **policy** obligations:

- ✅ **Privacy policy exists and is linked in the store listing** — `/privacy.html`.

## §5 — Pre-submission checklist

- [ ] That exact URL is entered in **both** places Play asks for it: the store listing and the
      Data Safety form.
- [ ] Open the URL on a device **with the PWA installed** — confirms the service-worker

      (`navigateFallbackDenylist` in `vite.config.js`).
- [ ] `Last updated` in `src/config/privacyPolicy.js` matches the date actually published.
- [x] App name in the manifest matches the name in the policy and the listing (§4) — done and
      guarded 2026-08-16. Enter `Tinku Math: Maths for Kids` as the listing title.
- [ ] **`OPERATOR_LINE` is still blank** — the policy names no operator. Decide own name vs. trade
      name (it must match the Play developer name), set it in `src/config/privacyPolicy.js`, run
      `npm run privacy:build`, and redeploy **before** submitting.
- [ ] Declare the target age band (**Ages 6–8** for Grades 1–3). The policy states no age, so
      Console is the only place it is asserted.
- [ ] Re-read §0 against the build being submitted. **If anything in §0 is no longer true, stop
      and re-answer this form.**
