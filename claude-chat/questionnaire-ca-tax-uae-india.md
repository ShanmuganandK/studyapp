# Written Questionnaire — Chartered Accountant / Tax Advisor (UAE ↔ India)
**Re: "Tinku Math" — paid children's app sold to Indian consumers by a UAE-resident solo founder**

## How to use this document
Designed for **written responses** — please answer beneath each numbered question.
Where the answer depends on thresholds or elections, please state the threshold and
what changes on crossing it. Where something is outside your scope (e.g. Indian
regulatory law), a one-line referral note is fine. Sections A–C are the decision-
gating ones; D–F can follow later.

## Factual background (please read before answering)

1. **Person:** individual founder, **Indian citizen**, currently resident in the
   UAE (i.e. NRI for Indian tax purposes, subject to your confirmation of the
   day-count tests). No Indian entity, no Indian office/employees/agents. Please
   answer all questions for this NRI profile specifically — and flag any answer
   that would change if my residential status shifted (e.g. RNOR on extended India
   stays, or return to India).
2. **Product:** a children's educational app. Distribution: Google Play Store,
   India market.
3. **Revenue model (planned):** auto-renewing subscriptions in ₹ (~₹99–149/month,
   ~₹499–999/year) via **Google Play Billing**. Google acts as merchant of record
   for India — collects from the customer, handles Indian GST as marketplace, remits
   proceeds to the developer after its ~15–30% fee. Payouts to a **UAE bank account**.
4. **Scale expectations:** ₹0 today; early revenue small (tens of thousands of ₹ per
   month); plan reconsiders structure at roughly ₹3–5 lakh/month.
5. **Costs:** cloud services (Google Firebase, likely billed to UAE), freelancers
   (possibly Indian, e.g. a teacher-reviewer paid a small one-off fee), design tools.
6. **Possible future:** direct web billing via an Indian gateway (Razorpay) *bypassing*
   Play Billing — only past the ~₹3–5L/month threshold; and possibly an Indian entity
   at some point.

## Section A — Where is this income taxed? (the core question)

**A1.** Subscription income from Indian consumers, collected by Google as merchant
of record, paid out to a UAE resident individual: under the India–UAE DTAA, is any
of this income taxable in **India** in my hands? Please address: business income
with no Permanent Establishment; whether app subscriptions could be characterised
as **royalties or Fees for Technical Services** under the treaty; and whether
Google's role changes the analysis.

**A2.** Does India's **Equalisation Levy / Significant Economic Presence** regime
(as currently in force) apply to me — a non-resident supplying an app to Indian
users via a marketplace? If any levy applies, who bears the compliance burden
(me vs. Google), at what rate, above what threshold?

**A3.** In the **UAE**: does this income fall within UAE Corporate Tax for a natural
person (business-income threshold AED 1M turnover), and at today's expected scale is
there any UAE registration/filing obligation at all? At what turnover do obligations
switch on, and what should I do *now* to be ready?

**A4.** Please state plainly: at launch scale, **what tax do I actually pay, where,
and what returns must I file in each country?** (The "do-nothing-wrong minimum"
picture.)

## Section B — GST / indirect tax

**B1.** For Play-Billing sales, my understanding is Google (as the marketplace /
OIDAR-style intermediary) collects and remits Indian GST on consumer sales, and I
need **no Indian GST registration** while all India revenue flows through Play
Billing. Is that correct? Any residual GST exposure or paperwork on my side
(e.g. invoices Google issues on my behalf)?

**B2.** If I later add **direct web billing via Razorpay** to Indian consumers: this
presumably makes me an OIDAR supplier requiring Indian GST registration and monthly
filings. Please outline the obligations, realistic compliance cost, and the revenue
level at which it becomes worth it — validating or correcting our working threshold
of ~₹3–5L/month.

**B3.** UAE VAT: are these sales (to Indian consumers, via Google) outside the scope
/ zero-rated exports for UAE VAT purposes, and is any UAE VAT registration needed
